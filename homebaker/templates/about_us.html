About HomeBaker

Bringing the sweetness of home-baked love to your doorstep.

Our Story

Welcome to HomeBaker, a platform dedicated to connecting passionate home bakers with cake enthusiasts. We believe that the best cakes aren't made in giant factories, but in home kitchens filled with love, tradition, and the finest ingredients.

What started as a vision to empower local talent has grown into a community where every celebration is made special with a custom touch. Whether it's a birthday, wedding, or just a Tuesday treat, our bakers are here to make your moments unforgettable.

College Project Milestone

This platform is a proud College Project developed with dedication and technical excellence. It was created by Avin CV and Dhijil Davis, reaching its significant development milestone on January 7th. This project showcases our commitment to building practical, user-centric web applications using modern technologies.

Our Mission

To provide a seamless experience for customers to discover unique, handcrafted cakes while providing home bakers with the tools and platform they need to grow their small businesses.

Empowering Homemakers

HomeBaker was built with a special purpose — to give unemployed housewives a powerful opportunity to earn money from the comfort of their own home.

If you have a passion for baking, you don't need a storefront or big investment. Simply register as a baker, list your creations, and start receiving orders. HomeBaker handles the platform so you can focus on what you love — baking!

Why HomeBaker is perfect for homemakers:
  • Work at your own pace — no fixed hours
  • No investment required — just your skills
  • Turn your hobby into a source of income
  • Reach customers directly from your kitchen
  • Be your own boss and build your brand
